- A new look across the whole app: clearer pages, a sectioned menu on desktop and a Dompet tab on phones.
- Laporan now shows a daily calendar, a spending trend against last month, where the money went, top places and the biggest expenses, plus a yearly view. A new Alur uang page shows money flowing from income to wallets to spending.
- Anggaran has its own page with budgets per category and a marker for how far into the month you are.
- Close the day from Beranda to keep a streak; set up recurring transactions that appear on their own; correct a wallet to its real balance with Penyesuaian saldo.
- Privacy settings: switch usage statistics off, see what you agreed to, and download all your data in one file.
- Plans and credits (test mode only) and a public site with an install guide.
