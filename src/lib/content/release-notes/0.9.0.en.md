- Pro look: with Pro or Agen you get the Emas and Hijau tua themes, a Pro badge, a crowned Koko, a framed monthly number on Beranda and a Pro home-screen icon (shown after you reinstall).
- New currencies: TWD, HKD and CNY (RMB). Amounts in currencies with cents now take cents, for example 12,50 in Indonesian or 12.50 in English; rupiah and yen stay whole numbers.
- OCBC Rekening Koran statements now import, including statements with a foreign-currency sub-account (only the rupiah part is imported). The PDF password field explains the OCBC date-of-birth password.
- Clearer messages when an amount has too many digits after the comma, and a refused amount stays as you typed it.
