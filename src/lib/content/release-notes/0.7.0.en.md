- More ways to import on Impor: Excel and Google Sheets files (.xlsx), plus scanned statements, mobile-banking and e-wallet screenshots and camera photos of paper statements. Photos and scans are read on your phone and never leave it, and no key is needed. Rows with doubtful digits wait for your check, and Tambah › Mutasi now opens Impor.
- Kamera and Galeri for every photo upload (receipts, items, collectibles): big photos are shrunk and turned upright on your phone, and a HEIC photo gets a clear how-to-fix message.
- Cleaner shop names and a category with a visible reason on every capture. Change it in one tap and Montra remembers it for that space; see and delete what it learned in Pengaturan › Pedagang. The extra AI step needs your own Anthropic key and is not switched on yet.
- Tanya, the assistant that answers from your own records, replaces the "Segera hadir" page. It is available once the AI settings and your own key are in place, and is not available to use yet.
- Otomatis from your mailbox: a private forwarding address for bank mails and a Gmail connection (read-only, Montra's own Google app, in testing for listed users first). Both become available once they are configured, and neither is available to use yet.
- Easier install on iPhone and Android, with step-by-step help in Pengaturan, and a full list of past releases under Pengaturan › Tentang. Montra now checks for updates by itself, and "Baru di Montra" appears once after you update. Also fixed: the saved notice on Riwayat shows only after the save is confirmed, and brand badges no longer appear for words like "ke GoPay".
