- Imported bank rows now get a real category from the bank's own codes (bank fees, tax, interest, ATM cash, card payments, transfers out and in). "Rapikan kategori" on Impor tidies older imported rows: you review first, and you can undo it.
- Impor now checks that each month's opening balance matches the previous month's closing balance, shows any gap per wallet, and can record the difference as a visible balance adjustment after you confirm.
- Patungan: split a bill with friends by name, equally or by exact amounts, link it to the payment you made, and only your share counts in your totals. Mark each person paid when the money arrives. Montra never moves money.
- Kekayaan has a net-worth chart (1 month to all time), an allocation bar, suggestions for what you have not counted yet, grouped wallets, investments and crypto, and credit-card spending for the last 30 days.
- Langganan can check your recent payments and suggest subscriptions you have not added yet.
- A streak page with a calendar, milestones and freezes, an evening reminder to close the day, a budget streak, and a cleaner Impor page that shows which banks can be read today.
