- Transfers between your own wallets (for example BCA to OCBC) no longer count twice. On Impor, "Match transfers" finds same-amount out and in pairs, you confirm each one, and every pair can be undone.
- Sign in with a passkey: fingerprint, face or device PIN. Add or remove passkeys in Settings, under Account.
- Smoother motion: page changes, charts that draw in, and pull to refresh in the installed app.
- Hiding amounts now also hides charts and percentages. A shop name the AI recognises appears as a "Use the name" suggestion instead of replacing the title on its own.
- Statement imports cope better with unusual notes, account numbers are masked more reliably, and Pro accounts no longer see the voucher card.
- Montra is now open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence.
