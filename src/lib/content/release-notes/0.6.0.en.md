- More banks on Impor: BNI, Mandiri (files with several accounts let you pick one) and OCBC e-statements now import; every row is checked against the printed balance, and a file that looks tampered with is refused whole.
- Meet Koko, Montra's little coin: it shows up on sign-in, empty pages and error pages, and cheers when a save, a closed day or a budget with money left is confirmed. Koko stays still when your phone asks for reduced motion.
- Badges for banks, e-wallets and ride or food apps on Dompet, Langganan, Beranda, Impor and Alur uang.
- Saving a receipt now needs that photo to be read first, and an offline delete sent twice no longer shows "Transaksi tidak ditemukan".
- Page titles no longer flicker when you scroll a little.
