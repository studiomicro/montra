- Money flow can now be viewed by month, quarter or year, with a comparison with the previous period and an average per month. A new net worth report shows the opening and closing value and the change per asset group.
- Home greets you by the time of day, with the comparison with last month and the budget left. Tap the date at the top to open a calendar of daily spending, closed days and bills.
- Every feature has its own icon, and a daily greeting built from your own figures can be turned off in Settings, under Appearance.
- Credit cards are now their own wallet: limit, statement and due days, the bill left to pay, and bill payments recorded as transfers.
