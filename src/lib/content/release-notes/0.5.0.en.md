- A new name: the app is now called Montra.
- Receipt reading: take a photo of a receipt and Montra reads the shop, date, total, items, tax and service; you check it before saving, as one transaction or per item. It uses your own Anthropic key.
- Pengaturan › Integrasi: store your own Anthropic key safely and test the connection.
- BCA statements: upload a BCA PDF e-statement on Impor; every row is checked against the printed balance before saving.
- Pengaturan › Tentang shows the version and what's new, and the app tells you when a new version is ready. Pengaturan › Pemakaian shows your plan, credits and the limits you have used.
