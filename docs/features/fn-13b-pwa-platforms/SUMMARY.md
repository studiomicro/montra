# FN-13b PWA platform compatibility (iOS and Android) — summary

**Status: complete** (2026-10-10). PRD: `PRD.md` (owner defaults A–I accepted automatically, owner 2026-10-10).
Branch `feat/fn-13b-pwa-platforms`, not merged. Risk tier Medium; no migration.

- **Goal:** the installed app behaves well on iPhone/iPad Safari and Android Chrome.
- **iOS:** `apple-mobile-web-app-title` and status-bar style `default` added (no "capable" tags: the manifest covers
  them). 36 portrait `apple-touch-startup-image` splash screens (18 sizes, light and dark) are generated by
  `scripts/generate-splash-screens.ts` from the icon and tokens. They are linked only for Apple user agents and not
  precached. `/pengaturan/instal` shows Bagikan › Tambah ke Layar Utama steps (server-rendered) and an "open in
  Safari" note for other iOS browsers. A one-time `InstallHint` is shown on iOS Safari only (localStorage
  `montra-install-hint-dismissed`). `/pengaturan/notifikasi` explains the iOS 16.4+ home-screen requirement.
  Inputs are ≥16 px globally, with user zoom kept. Safe-area insets come from the `--sai-*` variables on the header,
  tab bar, sheets, toasts, FABs and sidebar, and `dvh` is used.
- **Android:** manifest gets 4 shortcuts (Tambah transaksi, Suara, Struk, Tanya), narrow and wide screenshots from
  the demo seed (`scripts/capture-store-screenshots.ts`, local-only guard, Data demo ribbon), a maskable 192 icon,
  a monochrome icon, `categories` and `prefer_related_applications: false`. The theme toggle now updates the
  `theme-color` metas.
- **Assets added:** 733,570 bytes under `static/` (splash 144,851 B in 36 PNGs, screenshots 578,658 B in 3, icons
  about 10 KB).
- **Unchanged and re-verified:** CSP, `_headers`, offline page, FN-42/42b update flow. There is no share target
  (FN-15's job).
- **Tests:** `test/manifest.test.ts` and `test/platform-css.test.ts`, plus unit tests for user-agent, push,
  sw-policy, theme, splash and png. `e2e/pwa.spec.ts` was extended (iOS-UA steps, 360 px with notch emulation,
  input font sizes, manifest and meta checks).
- **QA:** PASS (1 cycle). **Red team:** PASS. LOW-1: anonymous HTML varies by User-Agent without `Vary`. LOW-2: the
  screenshot script uses `bypassCSP` and a fixed `.local` demo password, local only.
- **Not verified:** real iPhone/iPad and Android installs (PRD criteria 32–33). Not verified either: whether
  current iOS honours splash images and `theme-color` in standalone, real push delivery, and the double top inset
  on a notch device.
